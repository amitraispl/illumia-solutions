Client Overview

A nonprofit at scale, with stakes that match.

  • One of the largest nonprofit microfinance institutions in the Philippines, serving underserved communities across the country.
  • Distributed operations across multiple regions.
  • Handles sensitive financial and personal data.
  • Subject to regulatory and donor compliance.
  • Cost-sensitive yet security-critical environment.
The Challenge

Five gaps standing between today and resilience.

01

Limited Visibility

Lack of endpoints, network monitoring and visibility consistency across infrastructure.

02

Security Gaps

Weak processes, vulnerable endpoints, and limited compliance with framework requirements.

03

Weak Processes

Inadequate policies, procedures and internal vulnerability assessment processes.

04

Vulnerability Risks

Static security scans missed evolving threats and growing system risks.

05

Cost Constraints

Premium security tools required heavy investment with limited ROI clarity.
