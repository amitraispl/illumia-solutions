# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

IT directors, CIOs, and procurement leads at mid-to-large enterprises across Southeast Asia and globally. They're evaluating vendors for cybersecurity, cloud, or open-source infrastructure decisions — often in a committee or with a board. Secondary: non-profit and public-sector technology officers who need cost justification for open-source transition. They arrive with skepticism and leave decisions to budget cycles; first impressions determine whether a conversation happens.

## Product Purpose

Illumia Solutions is a managed IT services and consulting firm specialising in cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and open-source enterprise technology. The website is the primary trust and qualification surface — it must answer "are these people credible?" before a procurement conversation begins. Success is a contact form submission or a direct enquiry from a qualified decision-maker.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Authoritative. Grounded.

Not a startup. Not flashy. An expert firm that has done the work and has evidence to show for it. The voice is a senior consultant who knows what they're talking about and doesn't need to oversell.

## Anti-references

- Cheap IT vendor sites (logos, stock photo of handshakes, bullet lists)
- Hyperscaler landing pages (AWS, Azure) — too corporate-monolithic
- Generic SaaS "hero metric" designs — big rounded cards, gradient numbers, emoji icons
- Cybersecurity sites that lean on dark-neon-circuit-board aesthetics
- Startup-cool minimalism that reads as "no clients yet"

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Show the work. Numbers, client context, technical specifics. Trust is earned through detail, not stock imagery.
2. **Earned authority.** The tone is that of a firm that's delivered results — never breathless, never oversold.
3. **Precision in every detail.** Spacing, hierarchy, and copy are all intentional. Nothing looks accidental or AI-scaffolded.
4. **Each story is its own thing.** Case studies are individual narratives, not template instances. The client's context should be visible in the page itself.
5. **Quiet confidence over visual noise.** Restraint applied deliberately — not because we couldn't go further, but because we chose not to.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard navigable. Reduced-motion respected (GSAP conditional). Colour contrast checked across primary text.
