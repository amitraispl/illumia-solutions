# PRODUCT.md — Illumia Solutions Website

## Product Purpose
Marketing website for Illumia Solutions, a corporate IT consultancy offering enterprise cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and open-source solutions. The site is the primary sales and credibility asset for enterprise procurement teams across APAC, North America, and Africa.

## Users
Enterprise IT decision-makers and procurement teams at mid-to-large organizations. Secondary: compliance officers evaluating cybersecurity vendors. Primary geography: Philippines, India, USA, Australia, Kenya. These buyers are evaluating technical capability, compliance track record, and delivery model — not aesthetics.

## Register
brand

## Brand
- Name: Illumia Solutions ("illuminating the path to success")
- Tone: Authoritative, precise, globally capable with local roots
- Not: Startup-casual, SaaS-cute, corporate-generic

## Design Direction
Editorial/luxury tech aesthetic. Newsreader serif (font-headline) + Inter sans-serif (font-body). Primary red #b31c33, warm off-white #fcf9f8, dark stone neutrals. Inspired by editorial publications and precision-engineered brand identities. Explicitly NOT the cybersecurity dark-mode + neon defaults.

## Anti-References
- Dark navy with neon accents (Crowdstrike, Palo Alto aesthetic)
- SaaS-cream with gradient text and glassmorphism
- Generic MSP "checklist of services" websites
- Hero with stats row + 2 CTAs that look like every Tailwind landing page template

## Strategic Principles
- Differentiate from the "cybersecurity = dark/technical" default
- Lead with proof (case studies, compliance logos, client outcomes) not vague claims
- The "Illumia" brand metaphor (illumination, light) is underutilized
- Open-source positioning is a genuine differentiator vs proprietary-vendor competitors
