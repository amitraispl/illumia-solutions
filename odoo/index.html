Flagship Apps

The apps that carry a business.

CRM & Sales

A pipeline your sales team will actually use.

Drag-and-drop kanban pipeline with expected revenue per stage, activity scheduling, and built-in email and VoIP. Quotations generate from opportunities in two clicks, with e-signature and online payment on the quote itself — so closing a deal and booking the revenue are the same motion.

  • Kanban pipeline with stage revenue forecasts
  • Quotations with e-sign and online payment
  • Built-in email, VoIP, and activity scheduling
  • Lead scoring and automated assignment

Source: odoocdn.com

Healthcare

Patient records, billing, and inventory on one database.

Odoo runs hospital and clinic operations end to end — appointment scheduling, patient records, pharmacy and medical-supply inventory with lot/expiry tracking, and insurance billing — without stitching together separate systems for each department.

  • Appointment and patient record management
  • Pharmacy & medical inventory with expiry tracking
  • Insurance claims and patient billing
  • HR and payroll for clinical staff

Source: apps.odoocdn.com

Education

Admissions to alumni, run from a single platform.

Institutions use Odoo for admissions pipelines in CRM, fee invoicing and online payment, eLearning course delivery, and campus HR — with a parent/student portal reading live from the same records as the back office.

  • Admissions pipeline via CRM
  • Fee invoicing and online payment
  • eLearning app for course delivery
  • Student/parent self-service portal

Source: apps.odoocdn.com

Accounting

Accounting that keeps up with the rest of the suite.

Customer invoices, vendor bills, and bank feeds reconcile on one dashboard. AI-assisted bill digitisation reads PDFs into draft entries, and localisation packages cover 80+ countries' tax and reporting rules — including India GST. Every sale, stock move, and manufacturing order posts to the ledger automatically.

  • Bank feeds with smart reconciliation
  • AI bill digitisation from PDF and scan
  • 80+ country localisations, GST included
  • Real-time P&L, balance sheet, and aged reports

Source: odoocdn.com

Inventory

Warehouse logic that mirrors your floor.

Double-entry inventory — every stock move has a source and destination, so nothing vanishes. Multi-warehouse routes, push and pull rules, dropshipping, cross-docking, and barcode operations on any device. Replenishment fires automatically from reordering rules or make-to-order flows.

  • Double-entry stock moves, full traceability
  • Routes: dropship, cross-dock, multi-step
  • Barcode app for receipts, picks, and counts
  • Lot, serial, and expiry tracking

Source: odoocdn.com

Manufacturing

MRP, work centres, and a paperless shop floor.

Bills of materials with routings and work-centre capacity, master production scheduling, and a tablet-first shop-floor terminal that shows operators worksheets, quality checks, and time tracking. Maintenance, PLM, and Quality apps snap in when you need them.

  • Multi-level BOMs with routings and versions
  • Shop-floor tablet view with worksheets
  • Work-centre load and capacity planning
  • Integrated Maintenance, Quality, and PLM

Source: odoocdn.com

Point of Sale

Retail and restaurant POS that survives the network.

Browser-based POS that keeps selling offline and syncs when the connection returns. Shops, restaurants with floor plans and kitchen displays, self-order kiosks, and payment-terminal integrations — all posting straight into the same inventory and accounting as everything else.

  • Offline-first — sales continue without internet
  • Restaurant floor plans and kitchen display
  • Self-service kiosk and mobile ordering
  • Direct posting to stock and ledger

Source: odoocdn.com

Website & eCommerce

Drag-and-drop website builder, product pages, payment providers, and shipping connectors — the storefront reads stock and pricing live from the same database.

Marketing Suite

Email, SMS, and social campaigns with marketing automation flows triggered by CRM stages, cart abandonment, or any field in the system.

HR & People

Employees, recruitment pipeline, time off, appraisals, expenses, and fleet — employee self-service through the same portal.

Field Service & Helpdesk

SLA-driven tickets, on-site interventions with worksheets, planning Gantt for crews, and timesheets that flow into invoicing.
